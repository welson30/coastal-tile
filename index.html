How a job runs

Most tile problems start before the first tile goes down. This is the order we work in.

  1. Look at the space

    We review photos or visit, check the substrate and talk through the material, layout and schedule.

  2. Written estimate

    Scope, price and what's included in writing. Material supply is agreed job by job: you buy it, or we arrange it.

  3. Prep the surface

    Flat, sound and, in wet areas, waterproofed per the system manufacturer's requirements before tile is set.

  4. Lay out and set

    Layout planned from the focal points, so cuts land at the edges and grout lines run true.

  5. Finish and walk through

    Grout, trim and cleanup, then a walkthrough with you before we call it done.

We stand behind our work

The person who quotes your job is accountable for how it's installed.

  • Proper preparation, alignment, clean cuts and finishing
  • Straight answers about what a material or space can and can't do
  • Showing up when we say we will, and keeping you informed
  • Coming back to fix covered installation issues

We stand behind our workmanship and are committed to resolving covered installation issues in accordance with the applicable written warranty terms, provided with your estimate. Material defects are generally covered by the manufacturer or supplier. Damage from misuse, structural movement, other trades' work or improper maintenance is not an installation issue.
