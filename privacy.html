Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to the information you send through our estimate request form.

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number, project location, project details, any photos you attach and your preferred contact method.

Only to respond to your request, prepare an estimate and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Form submissions are delivered to our business email through an email delivery service. Our website host may keep standard technical logs. Photos are resized on your device before sending.
